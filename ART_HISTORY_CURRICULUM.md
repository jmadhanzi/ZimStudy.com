## 3. Module Details

### 3.1. Introduction to Art History
- Overview of global art traditions
- Key movements and styles
- Comparative study of art across continents

### 3.2. African Art & Architecture
- Pre-colonial African art (Benin bronzes, Nok terracottas)
- Shona stone sculpture tradition
- Rock Art (Matobo, Chinhoyi)
- African textiles, pottery, and metalwork
- Traditional and contemporary African architecture

### 3.3. Zimbabwean Art History
- Modern Shona sculpture movement
- Liberation-era art and protest art
- Contemporary Zimbabwean artists
- Zimbabwean art market and galleries

### 3.4. Western Art Traditions
- Classical Greek and Roman art
- Medieval and Byzantine art
- Renaissance masters (Leonardo, Michelangelo)
- Baroque and Rococo
- Impressionism and Post-Impressionism
- Modern art movements (Cubism, Surrealism)

### 3.5. Art Theory & Criticism
- Visual analysis methods
- Historical research methods
- Critical theory (Marxist, feminist, postcolonial)
- Semiotics and visual language

### 3.6. Visual Culture Studies
- History of photography
- Film aesthetics and visual storytelling
- Advertising and commercial art
- Visual symbolism and cultural meanings

### 3.7. Museum & Curatorial Studies
- Exhibition design and visitor experience
- Curation principles and themes
- Art conservation and preservation
- Art documentation and cataloging
- Museum management and operations

### 3.8. Contemporary Art & Global Trends
- Digital and new media art
- Performance and installation art
- Conceptual art and dematerialization
- International art markets and fairs
- Globalization and transnational art

### 3.9. Art of Religion & Ritual
- Religious iconography (Christian, Islamic, etc.)
- Sacred art and devotional objects
- Art in African Traditional Religion
- Christian art in African context
- Interpretation of sacred imagery

### 3.10. Aesthetics & Philosophy of Art
- Ancient aesthetics (Plato, Aristotle)
- Enlightenment aesthetics (Kant, Burke)
- Modern philosophy of art (Hegel, Nietzsche)
- Contemporary and postmodern aesthetics
- African aesthetics and Ubuntu
- Questions of artistic value and meaning
